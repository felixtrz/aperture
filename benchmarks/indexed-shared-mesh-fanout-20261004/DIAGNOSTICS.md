publication/mirroring and compiled packing/planning/conversion/execution, plus
separately labeled simulated GPU APIs and evidence records. All seven session
definitions and fourteen scene states are exercised on CPU. The browser module
preflight resolves and parses 950 current modules without opening a server or
browser. The optional audio import is proved disabled by the actual call/config
and guarding source, rather than silently omitted.

The source-only native observer, indirect decoder, their two adversarial suites
and original legacy geometry are retained byte-for-byte. This preparation adds
index consumption validation and identity indexing; it does not change the
underlying draw route or engine source.

will be checked against real submissions; CPU mocks do not establish them.
