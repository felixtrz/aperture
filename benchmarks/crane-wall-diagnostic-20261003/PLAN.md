# Bounded crane wall diagnostic

Fresh exact-byte A/B v2 reproduction precedes single-variable controls. Eight native attempts maximum, including failures. Cases 1–7 are fixed in cases.json. Conditional eighth removes exactly matched internal wall-face pairs from the no-shadow case with clearest persistent vertical seams; if seams disappear with shadows, it instead repeats an implicated control at the frozen rear camera. No engine edits or frozen-input modifications.

Each attempt is sequential through the adopted runtime_pressure lifecycle and runVerifiedScene. Source, dependency, original-input and capture hashes are recorded before/after. Reproduction mismatch halts for reconciliation. Free storage must stay at least 2 GiB; all evidence is retained.

Controls isolate: shadow contribution; fixed-sample PCF radius; normal-bias sensitivity; PCSS radius; and (conditionally) internal coincident closed-solid faces. Reduced shadow radius is not a quality-equivalent engine comparison. Bias improvement can trade acne for detached contact shadows. The front-focused budget does not prove any cause at the rear camera unless explicitly captured.
